## 1. Executive Vision & Core Philosophy

The **Property DNA Engine™** is the single, authoritative, multi-dimensional projection of a property’s identity, physical state, risks, and financial outlook in the Stratex ecosystem. Rather than serving as a separate database with independently authored facts, Property DNA is generated dynamically as a read-optimized, hash-chained projection of approved Passport intelligence records. 

```
 ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │           PASSPORT CANONICAL EVIDENCE LEDGER           │
 │ (Approved Findings, Certified Contractor Work, Missions)│
 └───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                             │ (Monotonic Ingestion & Mapping)
                             ▼
 ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │               PROPERTY DNA ENGINE™                     │
 │          (Dynamic Projection & Provenance)             │
 └───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
         ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                   ▼
   ┌───────────┐       ┌───────────┐       ┌───────────┐
   │  Habitat  │       │  Reports  │       │ Insurance │
   │ (Consume) │       │ (Display) │       │ (Future)  │
   └───────────┘       └───────────┘       └───────────┘
```

By enforcing a single schema and pipeline, Stratex eliminates the risk of fragmented state, duplicated or conflicting calculations, and "invented" details. Every metric, score, or classification in Property DNA has a verifiable chain of custody (provenance) back to the raw, human-approved or machine-verified findings in the Property Passport.

## 2. The Five Immutable Rules

### Rule #1: Not Another Database
Property DNA is **not** an independent, self-contained data store. It is a projected representation. The Property Passport remains the **only** canonical record. All writes are directed exclusively through the Passport; any direct mutations to Property DNA are strictly prohibited except via hardened Passport update channels.

### Rule #2: One Living Property DNA
Every property receives exactly one living, continuous Property DNA profile. It evolves dynamically. As new inspections are conducted, repairs completed, or renovations verified, the pipeline ingests these events, updates the DNA projection, incrementing its sequential version number while preserving the complete historic timeline.

### Rule #3: Guaranteed Provenance
Every single value within the Property DNA record MUST explicitly state its provenance. The ecosystem recognizes exactly four provenance categories:
1. **Verified:** Backed by an approved physical observation, physical sample, or certified contractor receipt with closeout validation.
2. **Estimated:** Generated by an approved engineering calculation, physical dimension formula, or calibrated sensor profile.
3. **Projected:** Produced by predictive models, decay curves (such as HVAC and roof lifespans), or historical macro-climatic data.
4. **Unknown:** Placed when there is zero physical or calculable evidence. **Never fabricate certainty.**

### Rule #4: The "Why?" Rule (Traceability)
Every single value in Property DNA links back to one or more specific Passport evidence IDs (such as finding IDs, mission IDs, or contractor invoices). Users looking at any DNA value must be able to click and immediately view the underlying evidence.

### Rule #5: Consuming the Same DNA
No application is permitted to build custom DNA projections, duplicate calculation logic, or maintain separate caching records. Habitat (homeowner portal), Reports (structural assessments), and future Insurance products must ingest the exact same JSON representation.

## 3. DNA Category Specification

The Property DNA is structured into seven high-density categories. Each property value within these categories is an object containing `current_value`, `provenance`, `confidence_score`, and `evidence_links`.

### A. Identity
Establishes the base attributes of the structure.
*   **Property Name:** The designated name (e.g., "Sandy Shores Cottage").
*   **Address:** Standardized geo-coded physical address.
*   **Build Year:** Verified construction year from public assessor records or historical blueprints.
*   **Size:** Gross heated/cooled square footage.
*   **Construction Type:** Outer framing material (e.g., Wood Frame, Steel Frame, Masonry).
*   **Climate Zone:** DOE climate zone division (e.g., Zone 3 - Moist).

### B. Health
The real-time physical condition of the property’s building envelopes and active systems.
*   **Overall Home Health:** Weighted composite score (0-100) aggregating the sub-components.
*   **Structural:** Integrity of load-bearing walls, joists, and structural members.
*   **Roofing:** Status of shingles, underlayment, valleys, and flashing.
*   **Exterior:** Wall cladding, trim, paint, and siding integrity.
*   **Moisture:** Water intrusion monitoring, relative humidity thresholds, and leak detection.
*   **Air:** Air tightness, ventilation rates, and indoor air quality indicators.
*   **Energy:** Overall efficiency, insulation coverage (R-values), thermal bridging.
*   **Safety:** Smoke/CO alarms, fire egress, electrical grounding, stair rail compliance.
*   **Site:** Grading, drainage run-off, retaining walls, vegetation clearance.

### C. AWE™ (Air, Water, Energy)
The strategic resilience and resource efficiency profile.
*   **Air Index:** Air exchange rates, envelope seal tightness, and ventilation efficiency.
*   **Water Index:** Drainage performance, water-damage resilience, and consumption profile.
*   **Energy Index:** Solar utilization, thermal barrier performance, and mechanical efficiency.
*   **Overall AWE Index:** Aggregated, system-wide resilience score.

### D. Risk
A forward-looking risk posture score based on local hazards and building age.
*   **Current Risk Level:** Real-time risk band (Low, Medium, Elevated, Critical).
*   **Five-Year Risk Projection:** Decayed risk analysis factoring in wear and climate trends.
*   **Deferred Maintenance Exposure:** Accumulation of unaddressed minor findings.
*   **Weather Exposure:** Susceptibility to local high-wind, hail, or seismic activity.
*   **Moisture Exposure:** Proximity to flood zones, vegetation overhang, or soil drainage issues.
*   **Insurance Exposure:** Expected insurability status based on structural components and roof age.

### E. Lifecycle
A ledger of key asset lifespans, trackable over time.
*   **Roof Age:** Age of current roof deck and covering.
*   **HVAC Age:** Service age of active mechanical heating/cooling equipment.
*   **Windows:** Material lifecycle and seal age.
*   **Exterior:** Siding or cladding paint/sealant age.
*   **Foundation:** Settlement tracking and structural aging.
*   **Solar:** Age and efficiency rating of solar arrays, inverters, and battery banks.
*   **Major Components:** Hot water heaters, electrical panels, and appliance lifespan.
*   **Remaining Service Life:** Overall system remaining operational lifespan prior to critical replacement requirements.

### F. Financial
The asset valuation and liability forecast layer.
*   **Estimated Deferred Maintenance:** Hard financial dollar amount required to bring the home back to a fully healthy baseline.
*   **Estimated Annual Energy Waste:** Calculated monetary loss due to poor thermal insulation, air leakage, and aged HVAC equipment.
*   **Completed Improvements:** Capital improvements logged and certified (increases home value and insurability).
*   **Future Investment Opportunities:** Recommended upgrades (e.g., "Heat Pump Upgrade", "R-49 Attic Blow-in") with ROI and rebate forecasts.

### G. History
Operational audit trail of professional touchpoints.
*   **Inspection Count:** Number of certified inspectors who have physically accessed the property.
*   **Mission Count:** Drone, camera, or thermal imaging scans successfully completed.
*   **Major Events:** Extreme weather survivals, major renovations, or change of ownership.
*   **Timeline Summary:** Chronological log of high-priority events.
*   **Verified Contractor Work:** Signed-off work orders and building permits.
*   **Warranty Status:** Active structural, mechanical, or roofing material warranties.

## 4. Engineering Law: Absolute Compliance

> **If Property DNA cannot explain where a value originated, it shall not display that value.**

### The Null-provenance Rendering Guard
Any client application (Habitat, Reports, Contractors, or Insurance platforms) displaying Property DNA must implement a rendering guard:
```javascript
function renderDnaValue(dnaField) {
  if (!dnaField.provenance || dnaField.provenance === "Unknown" || !dnaField.evidence_links || dnaField.evidence_links.length === 0) {
    // SUPPRESS DISPLAY entirely. Do not render placeholders or fabricate fake values.
    return null; 
  }
  return (
    <div className="dna-item">
      <span className="value">{dnaField.current_value}</span>
      <span className="badge-provenance">{dnaField.provenance}</span>
      <a href={`/passports/evidence/${dnaField.evidence_links[0]}`}>Why?</a>
    </div>
  );
}
```

This ensures the user is never presented with data that does not have an active backlink to the Passport evidence ledger.
