What STRATEX™ Is

STRATEX™ is the Strategic Thermal Reconnaissance & Topography Estimator — a dual-sided, hyper-secure B2B SaaS that pairs autonomous Mobile Drone Units (MDUs) with a multi-LLM AI verification stack to deliver centimeter-grade roof, gutter, and siding quotes in under 90 minutes, co-branded for QXO and your contractor partners. It is sold to building-material suppliers (you), then deployed downward to your contractor accounts as a no-cost loyalty utility that they cannot replicate independently.

THE HARDWARE

A truck-mounted enclosed-trailer Mobile Drone Unit (MDU) carrying a DJI Matrice 4TD with auto-dock, Starlink uplink, RTK GPS, and a custom hatch actuator. Drives to the address, launches autonomously, returns in 18 minutes with thermal + LiDAR + photogrammetry payload.

THE BRAIN

A two-pass Trifecta Verification Stack runs every measurement and every price through nine independent LLM panelists across three providers (Claude Sonnet 4.6 + GPT-5.4 + Gemini 3.1 Pro). Two adversarial rounds. 2-of-3 consensus required. Tamper-evident audit ledger.
